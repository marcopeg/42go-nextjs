# Add a Local Playback Settings Popover to the LingoCafe Reader

## Elevator's Pitch

Replace the LingoCafe reader player's current speed-cycling button with a gear-and-speed settings button. The button opens a compact playback settings popover containing five speech speeds plus positive toggles for word highlighting, sentence highlighting, translation auto-pause, and settings auto-pause. Every selection applies immediately and persists on the local device through browser storage.

## Business Gain

Readers can adjust voiceover pacing and visual guidance without leaving the book or visiting their profile. Keeping the controls inside the active player makes experimentation fast, while device-local persistence restores the preferred playback experience on later pages and sessions.

## Current State

The floating reader player renders a button labeled with the current speed, such as `1×`. Clicking it calls `playback.cycleSpeed`, which rotates through the existing `0.5×`, `0.75×`, `1×`, and `1.25×` speeds. The speed starts at `1×` for each new playback controller and is not stored.

Voiceover playback separately exposes an active sentence ID and a best-effort active word range. `BookPageReader` renders a background for the spoken sentence and a stronger treatment for the active word when the device/browser supplies usable boundary events. Neither highlight can currently be disabled. Playback also auto-pauses when the reader opens a sentence translation and resumes when translation closes.

The reader already has a local-storage preference pattern at `lingocafe.reader.preferences.v1`, while playback-position memory uses a separate playback-specific local-storage store.

## Desired State

The current speed-cycling control in the open floating player becomes a clearly identifiable settings button. Activating it opens a compact, accessible popover anchored to the player.

The settings trigger shows a gear icon together with the current speed, such as `1×`, so the compact control communicates both its purpose and active playback rate.

With `Auto-pause on settings` enabled, opening the popover pauses active playback so the reader can change settings without speech continuing underneath it. The active sentence and utterance position remain stable. Closing the popover automatically resumes only playback that the popover auto-paused. If only highlighting or auto-pause toggles changed, the paused utterance continues from its mid-sentence position. If speed changed, the current sentence restarts at the selected speed. Turning `Auto-pause on settings` off lets playback continue while the popover is open and prevents the popover from automatically resuming playback on close.

The popover contains five rows:

1. A Speed row presenting five choices: `0.25×`, `0.5×`, `0.75×`, `1×`, and `1.25×`. Selecting a speed marks and persists it immediately. If settings auto-pause is active, closing the popover restarts the current sentence at the new speed. If settings auto-pause is disabled and speech is continuing beneath the popover, selecting a speed immediately cancels the current utterance and restarts that same sentence at the new speed.
2. A `Word highlighting` row with a toggle. Turning it off immediately removes or suppresses the active spoken-word treatment while leaving sentence highlighting available.
3. A `Sentence highlighting` row with a toggle. Turning it off immediately removes or suppresses the playback sentence background while leaving word highlighting available.
4. An `Auto-pause on translation` row with a toggle. Turning it off prevents translation interactions from automatically pausing or resuming playback.
5. An `Auto-pause on settings` row with a toggle. Turning it off prevents the settings popover from automatically pausing or resuming playback.

Changing a speed or toggle keeps the popover open so the reader can adjust several settings in one visit. The popover closes through an explicit close control, outside click/tap, Escape, or by clicking the gear/speed trigger again. Every close path applies the same accepted resume behavior: continue a paused utterance from its current position when speed did not change, or restart the current sentence when speed changed.

The speed and all four positive boolean settings persist together as device-local playback preferences in local storage. They load when the reader initializes and remain effective while navigating between book pages or returning in a later browser session on that device. Missing, invalid, or legacy storage values resolve safely to `1×` speed with all four boolean settings set to `true`.

The four highlight combinations remain independent: both visible, sentence only, word only, or neither. Disabling visual cues must not remove internal active-sentence state used for speech sequencing, progress, seeking, or guided scrolling. These settings must not affect translation selection highlighting or translation behavior.

No data is written to `auth.users.profile`, the shared profile API, the database, or the session. Cross-device, cross-browser, and cross-tab synchronization is out of scope.

## Definition of Success

A reader can open the player settings popover, select any existing speed, independently turn either playback highlight or either automatic pause behavior on or off, see each change take effect immediately, and get the same settings back after reloading or reopening the reader on the same browser device.

## Additional Context

This task was initially drafted as a LingoCafe profile feature. During clarification, the operator replaced that design with a player-local settings experience. The earlier profile Save decision is superseded and must not guide implementation.

The current playback speed options come from `READER_PLAYBACK_SPEEDS`. The new UI should select directly from those values instead of cycling blindly. The local-storage approach should follow the defensive parsing and fallback behavior already used by reader preferences without necessarily coupling visual reading preferences and playback preferences into the same storage object.

## Assumptions

- All five settings—speed and the four positive boolean toggles—are persisted locally.
- The accepted speed choices are exactly `0.25×`, `0.5×`, `0.75×`, `1×`, and `1.25×`.
- The settings trigger appears in the open floating playback control where the speed button exists today and combines a gear icon with the current speed.
- Default speed remains `1×`; `Word highlighting`, `Sentence highlighting`, `Auto-pause on translation`, and `Auto-pause on settings` all default to `true`, preserving existing behavior.
- Word highlighting remains best effort because device/browser speech boundary support varies.
- A dedicated versioned playback-preferences local-storage key is acceptable unless implementation review identifies a better established reader store boundary.
- Prefer a dedicated versioned playback-preferences storage key rather than mixing playback behavior into theme-specific visual reader preferences.
- Changes affect the current local app instance immediately; no storage-event synchronization with other tabs is required.

## Constraints

- Use the shared 42go Modal component only if the chosen interaction becomes a dialog, sheet, or overlay. A true anchored popover should use the repository's established popover primitive.
- Keep the settings usable in the compact mobile floating player without overflowing the viewport or colliding with safe-area insets.
- The popover must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, accessible names, and correct selected/toggle state semantics.
- Validate and sanitize local-storage data before use. Unsupported speeds and non-boolean toggle values must fall back safely.
- Do not store these settings in the user profile, database, cookies, or server session.
- Keep the playback speed set limited to the five accepted values.
- Do not confuse playback sentence highlighting with the separate translation selection highlight.
- Do not stop or alter speech sequencing, seeking, progress memory, or guided scrolling merely because a visual highlight is disabled.
- Preserve current reader behavior when local storage is unavailable, malformed, or throws.
- Run `npm run qa` after implementation and visually verify through the LingoCafe test URL.

## Dos

- Reuse `READER_PLAYBACK_SPEEDS` as the single source of truth for speed choices.
- Give the playback controller explicit setters for speed, highlight preferences, and auto-pause preferences instead of retaining only the current `cycleSpeed` interface.
- Centralize playback preference defaults, sanitization, loading, and persistence.
- Preserve active playback state internally even when sentence or word styling is visually suppressed.
- Keep the popover compact and touch-friendly.
- Keep the popover open across setting changes; close it through the explicit close control, outside interaction, Escape, or a second click on the gear/speed trigger.
- When settings auto-pause is enabled, pause active playback when the settings popover opens and resume it on close while preserving its current sentence and utterance position.
- Test changes during active playback, including when a word boundary is currently visible.
- Cancel and restart only the current utterance when speed changes; keep the active sentence and playback position stable.

## Don'ts

- Do not retain click-to-cycle behavior on the settings trigger.
- Do not add these values to `auth.users.profile` or `LingocafePreferences`.
- Do not add a Save button to the playback popover.
- Do not implement one master highlight switch in place of two independent toggles.
- Do not combine the two auto-pause behaviors into one toggle.
- Do not couple sentence suppression to word suppression.
- Do not remove translation highlighting when playback sentence highlighting is disabled.
- Do not broaden the task into voice selection, additional speed values beyond the accepted five, cloud synchronization, or a general reader-settings redesign.
