## Decisions

- Keep the existing sentence-anchored translation popover. The shared 42Go Modal does not support anchoring to an inline sentence/word target and would break the reader interaction contract.
- Reuse the existing reader speech provider and playback controller for both continuous sentence playback and isolated word pronunciation.
- Keep isolated word pronunciation out of the playback-bar presentation and position model.
- Restart a manually paused sentence from its beginning on the next manual Resume after isolated word pronunciation cancels the browser utterance.
- Keep the translation action available during loading and error states whenever the device has full playback capability.
- Treat `Listen from here` as an explicit seek: it always starts from the translated sentence, including when opening translation auto-paused playback on another sentence.
- Keep ordinary sentence-popover dismissal unchanged so it resumes playback that translation auto-paused.
