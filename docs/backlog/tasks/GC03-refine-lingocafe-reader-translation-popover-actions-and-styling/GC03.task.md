# Refine LingoCafe Reader Translation Popover Actions and Styling

## Elevator's Pitch

Polish the LingoCafe reading-page translation popover so it uses the app primary color, closes when the reader taps the displayed translation, and clearly separates one-shot pronunciation from continuous playback.

## Business Gain

Keep translation and listening interactions fast and obvious without pulling readers out of the text. Readers should be able to dismiss a translation naturally, hear only the focused sentence or word for pronunciation practice, or deliberately continue playback from that location.

## Current State

The reader translation popover is implemented in `src/app/(app)/(lingocafe)/books/_components/BookPageReader.tsx`. It uses reader-specific background and border tokens and currently renders its listening control as a full-height action on the right. Sentence scope offers `Listen from here`, which closes the popover and starts continuous playback from the selected sentence. Word scope offers `Play word`, which pronounces only the selected word and leaves the popover open. Taps inside the popover are treated as internal interactions, so tapping the translated text does not dismiss it.

## Desired State

Give the popover a subtle primary-color treatment: a very light primary-derived background, a stronger primary mix for borders and hover/focus feedback, and normal reader foreground colors for readable text. Apply the same design intent in light, dark, and system themes, adjusting mix strengths where contrast requires it.

Make the translated-content area dismissible. Tapping or clicking successful translation text, loading content, or error content closes the popover and clears the active translation highlight. The language-selection form remains interactive and does not close merely because its form area is tapped. Listening controls perform their own actions without triggering content-area dismissal.

Replace the current full-height right-side listening action with one compact bottom action line:

- Align the translation cache-source indicator (`API`, `DB`, `MEM`, or `CL`) to the left and vertically align it with the bottom of the action line.
- Align two small listening buttons horizontally on the right.
- Label the one-shot button `Play sentence` in sentence scope and `Play word` in word scope.
- Label the continuous-playback button exactly `Start audiobook from here` in both scopes.

The one-shot action speaks only the focused sentence or word. It does not open, start, seek, advance, or otherwise activate the continuous audiobook session or playback bar. The popover remains open after one-shot pronunciation so the reader can replay or study the translation.

`Start audiobook from here` closes the popover and starts continuous playback from the focused sentence. In word scope it starts from the word's containing sentence because continuous playback remains sentence-based.

While isolated sentence or word pronunciation is active, its link action changes to a filled button as a visible playing indicator. The audiobook action uses the same filled treatment for 600 ms, then the popover closes and continuous playback starts or resumes.

If opening the translation auto-paused an active continuous session, a one-shot action keeps that session paused while the popover remains open and resumes it only when the popover is dismissed. If playback was already manually paused, it remains manually paused after dismissal. If no continuous session existed, one-shot pronunciation returns to idle. Because browser speech has one active utterance, resuming an interrupted sentence may restart that sentence from its beginning.

## Definition of Success

On mobile and desktop, the translation popover visually belongs to the active LingoCafe theme, dismisses when its content is tapped without breaking its controls, and presents two compact, unambiguous listening choices: hear only the focused unit, or start the audiobook from that location. One-shot pronunciation never starts the continuous playback session. Starting the audiobook always closes the popover.

## Additional Context

This task revises behavior delivered by QN48. Preserve the existing reader translation scopes, speech-provider capability checks, translation loading/error/language-selection states, auto-pause rules, outside dismissal, Escape dismissal, analytics privacy, and responsive sentence anchoring unless the new interaction requires a narrowly scoped adjustment. The primary tint should use theme tokens or `color-mix()` rather than a hard-coded brand color.

## Assumptions

- `Start audiobook from here` is the exact continuous-playback label in sentence and word translation scopes.
- In word-translation scope, `Start audiobook from here` starts continuous playback from the word's containing sentence because continuous playback is sentence-based.
- Tapping the translated-content area dismisses the popover, while pressing a button inside the popover performs that button's action without being swallowed by the dismiss handler.
- One-shot sentence pronunciation should reuse the configured book-language voice and playback speed, as isolated word pronunciation does today.
- A subtle primary tint must remain readable in light, dark, and system reader themes.
- The existing cache-source values remain unchanged; only their placement moves into the bottom action line.

## Constraints

- Limit changes to the LingoCafe reader translation-popover and playback integration.
- Use existing primary and reader theme tokens; do not hard-code a LingoCafe color.
- Preserve accessible button semantics, keyboard focus indicators, and usable touch targets.
- Do not let translation-content dismissal interfere with language selection, listening buttons, or other interactive controls.
- Do not start or expose the full playback bar when `Play sentence` or `Play word` is used as a one-shot action.
- Keep the popover open after either one-shot action finishes.
- Close the popover when `Start audiobook from here` is activated.
- Avoid overlapping speech and preserve current playback pause/resume safety.
- Keep analytics free of translated or source text.
- Run `npm run qa` after implementation and verify through the LingoCafe test tunnel on mobile and desktop.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The translation popover uses a subtle primary-derived background treatment with matching border and interaction states.
- The primary treatment is readable and visually coherent in light, dark, and system reader themes.
- Clicking or tapping successful translation text, loading content, or error content closes the popover and clears its active translation highlight.
- Clicking or tapping inside the language-selection form does not dismiss the popover.
- Clicking or tapping a popover action does not accidentally trigger translation-content dismissal before the action runs.
- The popover has one compact bottom line with the cache-source indicator aligned left and the listening buttons aligned right.
- The two listening buttons are small, displayed horizontally, and ordered together on the right side of the bottom line.
- Sentence scope exposes a separate `Play sentence` one-shot action.
- Word scope exposes a separate `Play word` one-shot action.
- Sentence and word scopes expose a continuous action labeled exactly `Start audiobook from here`.
- `Play sentence` speaks only the focused sentence once.
- `Play word` speaks only the focused word once.
- The active one-shot listening action remains visibly filled for the duration of isolated speech and clears on completion, cancellation, or error.
- Neither one-shot action opens, starts, seeks, advances, or otherwise activates the continuous playback session or playback bar.
- The popover remains open after `Play sentence` or `Play word` finishes.
- `Start audiobook from here` closes the popover.
- `Start audiobook from here` shows filled feedback for 600 ms before the popover closes and playback starts or resumes.
- `Start audiobook from here` starts continuous playback from the focused sentence, or the containing sentence when the focused translation scope is a word.
- When translation auto-paused a continuous session, one-shot pronunciation keeps it paused while the popover is open and dismissal resumes it, restarting the interrupted sentence from its beginning when required by the browser speech provider.
- Playback that was already manually paused remains manually paused after one-shot pronunciation and popover dismissal.
- With no continuous session, one-shot pronunciation returns playback to idle without opening the playback bar.
- Listening controls are shown only when the existing speech capability and safe playback-state rules allow them.
- Loading, success, error, and translation-language selection states remain usable.
- Outside tap/click, active-target toggle, scrolling behavior, and Escape dismissal do not regress.
- The popover remains usable and correctly anchored on desktop and near a 390px mobile viewport.
- `npm run qa` passes.

- Do place the cache-source indicator and both compact actions on one balanced bottom line.

## Don'ts

- Don't hard-code a primary color value.
- Don't make the entire popover dismiss in a way that breaks its buttons or language selector.

- Don't broaden this task into reader-wide visual redesign or playback-settings changes.
