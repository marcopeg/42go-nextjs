### 1. What should happen to the translation popover after the user presses its Play action?

The cleanest default is to close the popover, then start or resume playback. This removes the translation auto-pause reason and lets the reader see the spoken sentence and playback highlighting. The alternative is to keep the popover open while speech runs.

The state-aware label is the clearest, but it requires the popover to know why playback is paused.

### 4. When playback is already speaking and translation auto-pause is disabled, should the popover action do anything?

The main alternatives are:

- show a disabled/currently-playing state;
- hide the action while playback is actively speaking;
- keep it actionable and restart playback from the tapped sentence.

Answer:

### 5. Should the Play action be visible during every translation state?

The translation popover can be loading, successful, or failed. One option is to show the playback action in all three states because listening does not depend on the translation request. Another is to show it only after a successful translation.

Answer:

always visible.
(only if the device supports it!)

### 6. What should the popover show when playback is unavailable?

Playback may be unavailable because speech synthesis is unsupported, the book has no playback language, no matching voice is installed, or the page has no readable sentences. Choose whether the action should:

- be hidden;
- remain visible but disabled, with the reason exposed through supporting text or a tooltip;
- remain enabled and surface the existing playback error only after it is pressed.

Answer:

hidden if the device does not support it.

### 7. Confirm the single-word behavior.

For a single-word translation, the action will speak from the beginning of the word’s containing sentence. It will not synthesize only the word and will not seek to a word offset.
