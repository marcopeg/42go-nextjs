# Refinement Questions v3 — Add a Play Action to the Reader Translation Popover

Answer inline under the question, or reply in chat, then say `next round`.

## Questions

### 1. What should be restored after `Play word` interrupts an existing paused utterance?

The browser speech provider has one active utterance. To pronounce the word, it must cancel the paused sentence; it cannot preserve an exact mid-sentence cursor and speak the word independently.

Please accept or change this proposed matrix:

- Continuous playback was auto-paused by translation: cancel the paused utterance, play the word once, then automatically restart the previously active sentence from its beginning and continue playback.
- Continuous playback was already manually paused: cancel the paused utterance, play the word once, then keep the playback bar paused on the same sentence; the next manual Resume restarts that sentence from its beginning.
- No continuous playback session existed: play the word once and return to idle without opening the playback bar.

Answer:

`Play word` must not close the single-word translation popover. The popover remains open after the word is pronounced and closes only through the normal translation dismissal interactions, such as tapping the active translation target again, tapping outside, or pressing Escape.

If continuous playback had been running and the translation interaction auto-paused it, keep it paused for as long as the word popover remains open. When the popover closes, resume that playback. Because the paused utterance was canceled to pronounce the word, resume by restarting the previously active sentence from its beginning.

If the continuous session was already manually paused, closing the word popover must not auto-resume it. If no continuous session existed, closing the popover leaves playback idle.
