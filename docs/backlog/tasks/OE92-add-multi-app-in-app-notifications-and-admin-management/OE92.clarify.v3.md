# Clarify Round v3 — Add Multi-App In-App Notifications and Admin Management

## Questions

### 1. Should the domain use communication, channel, and interaction as separate concepts?

The recommended vocabulary is a root `communication`, a delivery `channel`
such as `in_app` or future `email`, and an `interaction` such as `notice`,
`agreement`, `poll`, or `input`. Severity remains an independent presentation
dimension. This avoids overloading one `type` column with unrelated meanings.

### 2. Can one communication combine choices and open text?

The new example could mean either choosing poll *or* input, or showing both
structured options and an open answer in one communication. The recommended
extensible model is a validated response schema containing one or more ordered
fields, initially `choice` and `text`. The friendly backoffice presets can
still be Notice, Agreement, Poll, and Input, while the storage model can later
support a poll plus an optional “Other” text field without a migration.

### 3. How much email support belongs in the first release?

The recommended boundary is to include `channel` and channel-specific validated
configuration in the database design now, but implement only `in_app`
authoring, delivery, and response behavior in this task. Email template
selection, publishing, third-party pickup state, and delivery reporting should
be a later task; the sender itself remains external.

## Answers

### 1.

Accepted. Use `communication` as the root domain, `channel` for delivery such
as `in_app` or `email`, and `interaction` for the in-app response behavior.
Severity remains independent.

### 2.

Polls may optionally add one free-answer field alongside their configured
choice options. The administrator selects one of three modes: no free answer
(default), short input, or long textarea. This is poll-specific configuration;
the first release does not need an arbitrary multi-field form builder.

### 3.

Email authoring and publication are in the first release, but consumption and
sending are not. The backoffice can create an email communication with subject,
text, and the same normalized allow/deny audience targeting, then publish it.
Published email records remain available for a future external consumer but
are never returned to the in-app user component.

The `+ New` control is an intention-first menu with choices such as
Notification, Poll, Input, and Email. The selected kind determines the relevant
editor and is immutable from creation; an email cannot later become a poll.

## Resolution Summary

- Storage separates communication, channel, interaction, and severity.
- Polls support optional short or long free answers in addition to choices;
  no free answer is the default.
- The backoffice can author and publish unconsumed email records. The initial
  kind is selected from the `+ New` menu and cannot be changed.
