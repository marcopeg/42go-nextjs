## Questions

### 1. What should the active button show while pronunciation is running?

Recommended: keep the visible `Play sentence` / `Play word` label stable so the compact action line does not shift, replace the static play triangle with a small animated three-bar speaking indicator, and expose `Stop sentence` / `Stop word` as the accessible name while active.

Alternative: visibly change the label to `Stop sentence` / `Stop word` and use a stop icon or speaking animation, accepting or compensating for the button-width change.

Answer:

agreed with recommendation

### 2. When should the speaking indicator become active?

Recommended: activate it immediately after the speech request is accepted, covering any short device-voice startup delay, then clear it on natural completion, explicit stop, cancellation, or failure. This gives instant feedback without pretending to show percentage progress.

Alternative: activate it only when the speech provider reports that audible speech has started, leaving the button visually idle during voice startup.

Answer:

agreed with recommendation

### 3. If continuous audiobook playback was auto-paused when the translation opened, what should pressing the active pronunciation button to stop do?

Recommended: stop only the isolated sentence or word. Keep the audiobook paused while the translation remains open, then let the existing popover-dismissal behavior decide whether to resume it.

Alternative: stop the isolated pronunciation and immediately resume the auto-paused audiobook even though the translation remains open.

Answer:

agreed with recommendation
