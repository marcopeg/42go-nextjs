# Plan — Configure Tap-to-Translate Scope in LingoCafe Reader

## Goal

Let readers choose sentence or single-word tap translation in Reading Preferences, defaulting safely to sentence mode and keeping the scope independent of reader theme.

## Milestones

### Milestone 1 — Model a safe, theme-independent translation preference

- [x] Step 1 — Extend and sanitize the reader preference store
  - Achieve: Define a `sentence` / `word` translation-scope value with sentence as the fallback, and persist it at reader level rather than inside light/dark/system appearance profiles.
  - Create: none.
  - Modify: `src/app/(app)/(lingocafe)/books/_components/reader-preferences.ts`; `src/app/(app)/(lingocafe)/books/[bookId]/[pageId]/page.tsx`.
  - Delete: none.
  - Touch points: Existing local-storage migration/sanitization; reader settings event payload; theme-profile switching; the current reset behavior should remain appearance-scoped and must not accidentally couple translation granularity to a theme profile.
  - Validation: Verify empty, legacy, malformed, and newly saved preference stores resolve to sentence mode unless an explicit valid word mode is stored; verify the selection survives reload and theme changes.
  - Notes: The preference is stored as `translationScope` at reader level and is not sent to or stored by the API/database. Reset remains appearance-scoped.
