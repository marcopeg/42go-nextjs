## Questions

### 1. Current-mode signal

At the 56 px FAB size, what should make the active mode obvious at a glance: a short text label such as `Word` / `Sentence`, a compact `W` / `S` badge, or two distinct word-versus-sentence icons?

Answer:

Keep the translation icon and show the active mode as the string `word` or `sentence`. On desktop, show a popover explaining: `Click here to switch translation mode to …`.
